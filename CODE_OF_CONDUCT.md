# Code of Conduct

fLLM is a small technical project. This page says, in plain words, how we expect people to treat each other in issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other space run by the project.

## What we expect

* Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
* Assume good faith. Many contributors are learning, and English is not everyone's first language.
* Keep discussion technical and on topic. Give reproducible details when you report a problem and be patient when you wait for an answer.
* Accept review comments and criticism of your work without taking them personally, and give them kindly.
* Respect other people's privacy: do not post private information about anyone without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

* Personal attacks, insults, harassment, threats or sustained hostile behaviour.
* Discriminatory or demeaning remarks about a person or a group.
* Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, and deliberately disruptive posts.

## Reporting a problem

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the repository's private contact mechanism: open a private security
advisory on GitHub (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and say that it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer through
his GitHub profile. Do not post it in a public thread. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Consequences

The maintainer decides. Depending on the severity and whether it repeats, the outcome can be a reminder, a warning, hiding or
removing comments, or removal from the project's spaces (blocking from issues, pull requests and discussions).

This applies to every project space and to people who represent the project elsewhere.
